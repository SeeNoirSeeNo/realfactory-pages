🚀 RealFactory — Quick Start

A factory-building idle game played in Twitch chat • twitch.tv/seeno

📡 What happened?
Your spaceship crash-landed on an uncharted planet. Three machines were damaged in the crash. The ship's reactor is still running — but it burns wood as fuel. If wood runs out, the ship goes dark. Repair your machines and rebuild your factory to survive.

💬 How to Play

Type commands in Twitch chat to control your factory.
The overlay on stream shows your factory card — watch it update as you build!
Type !factory to get a link to your personal factory page.

⚡ First Steps

1
Join the game
!join
Your ship lands and three machines spawn — all damaged. You start with wood, stone, iron plates and repair packs.
2
Repair your Tree Farm
!repair tree_farm
The Tree Farm produces wood to keep your ship's reactor running. This is your most urgent repair.
Tree Farm
⚙️ — → 1x Wood every 30s
💰 25x Stone + 25x Iron Plate • 🔧 Repair: 1x Repair Pack
3
Repair your Iron Miner
!repair iron_miner
Once the wood supply is stable, get iron ore flowing.
Iron Miner
⚙️ — → 1x Iron Ore every 60s
💰 25x Iron Plate + 25x Wood • 🔧 Repair: 1x Repair Pack
4
Repair your Iron Smelter
!repair iron_smelter
Smelt iron ore into iron plates — the backbone of everything you'll build.
Iron Smelter
⚙️ 2x Iron Ore → 1x Iron Plate every 60s
💰 25x Stone + 5x Iron Plate • 🔧 Repair: 1x Repair Pack
5
Build a Stone Quarry
!build stone_quarry
Stone is needed for many buildings. Get a steady supply going.
Stone Quarry
⚙️ — → 1x Stone every 60s
💰 25x Iron Plate + 25x Copper Plate • 🔧 Repair: 1x Repair Pack
6
Build a Copper Miner
!build copper_miner
Copper unlocks the next tier of production. You'll need it for gears, belts and more.
Copper Miner
⚙️ — → 1x Copper Ore every 60s
💰 25x Iron Plate + 25x Wood • 🔧 Repair: 1x Repair Pack
7
Build a Copper Smelter
!build copper_smelter
Turn copper ore into copper plates to start building advanced machines.
Copper Smelter
⚙️ 2x Copper Ore → 1x Copper Plate every 60s
💰 25x Stone + 5x Iron Plate • 🔧 Repair: 1x Repair Pack
