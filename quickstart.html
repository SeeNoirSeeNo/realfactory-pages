🚀 RealFactory — Quick Start

A factory-building idle game played in Twitch chat • twitch.tv/seeno

What happened?
Your spaceship crash-landed on an uncharted planet. Three machines were damaged in the crash — your Tree Farm, Iron Miner and Iron Smelter all need repairs before they can run.

The ship's reactor is still running but it burns wood as fuel. You start with 3 Repair Packs in your inventory — one for each damaged machine. Once those are used up, any new machines have to be built from scratch using resources.

How to Play

Type commands in Twitch chat to control your factory.
The overlay on stream shows your factory card — watch it update as you build!
Type !factory anytime to get a link to your personal factory page.

First Steps

1
Join the game
!join
Your ship lands and three damaged machines spawn alongside it. You start with 50 Wood, 50 Stone, 75 Iron Plates, 25 Copper Plates and 3 Repair Packs.
2
Repair your Tree Farm
!repair tree_farm
Uses 1 Repair Pack. The Tree Farm produces wood to keep your ship's reactor running. This is your most urgent repair — without wood the ship loses power.
Tree Farm
Production— → 1x Wood (every 30s)
Build cost25x Stone + 25x Iron Plate
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
3
Repair your Iron Miner
!repair iron_miner
Uses 1 Repair Pack. With wood secured, get iron ore flowing. You'll need iron plates for everything you build going forward.
Iron Miner
Production— → 1x Iron Ore (every 60s)
Build cost25x Iron Plate + 25x Wood
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
4
Repair your Iron Smelter
!repair iron_smelter
Uses your last Repair Pack. Smelt iron ore into iron plates. That's all 3 repair packs spent — from here on, new machines must be built from scratch with resources.
Iron Smelter
Production2x Iron Ore → 1x Iron Plate (every 60s)
Build cost25x Stone + 5x Iron Plate
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
5
Build a Stone Quarry
!build stone_quarry
Your first build from scratch. Stone is needed for many machines. The ship has enough power for this.
Stone Quarry
Production— → 1x Stone (every 60s)
Build cost25x Iron Plate + 25x Copper Plate
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
6
Build a Copper Miner
!build copper_miner
Copper unlocks the next tier of production — gears, belts, inserters and more all need copper plates.
Copper Miner
Production— → 1x Copper Ore (every 60s)
Build cost25x Iron Plate + 25x Wood
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
7
Build a Copper Smelter
!build copper_smelter
Turn copper ore into copper plates. This is the last machine your ship can power on its own.
Copper Smelter
Production2x Copper Ore → 1x Copper Plate (every 60s)
Build cost25x Stone + 5x Iron Plate
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
8
Build a Biomass Generator
!build biomass_generator
Your ship's reactor can only power so many machines. The Biomass Generator burns wood to produce extra power — build one to keep expanding your factory.
Biomass Generator
Production1x Wood → 200 Power
Build cost15x Stone + 30x Iron Plate
Repair cost1x Repair Pack
9
You're on your own now!
The tutorial is complete. Expand your factory, upgrade your machines, and climb the leaderboard. Type !hint anytime for suggestions, or !build? to see what you can build next. Good luck!
